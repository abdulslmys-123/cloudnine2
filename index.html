Our Story

Where every cup
tells a story

Cloud Nine was born in Sheikh Zayed from one conviction: great coffee should be an experience, not just a habit. We set out to build a space — warm, unhurried, and honest — where the ritual of coffee is celebrated properly.

From our Spanish Latte (a firm crowd favourite) to our meticulously brewed filtered coffee, every drink is crafted with the same intention: make you feel something.

We now have three locations across Sheikh Zayed, and every one of them carries the same cosy, welcoming energy that earned us 4.6★ and 209 Google reviews.
